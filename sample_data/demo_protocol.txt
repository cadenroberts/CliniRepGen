CLINICAL TRIAL PROTOCOL

STUDY TITLE
A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel-Group Study to Evaluate 
the Efficacy and Safety of Drug X 10mg Once Daily in Adult Patients with Essential Hypertension

PROTOCOL NUMBER: DX-HT-301
VERSION: 2.0
DATE: January 15, 2023

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Background and Rationale
Essential hypertension affects approximately 1.3 billion adults worldwide and is a major 
risk factor for cardiovascular disease, stroke, and chronic kidney disease. Despite the 
availability of multiple antihypertensive therapies, many patients fail to achieve adequate 
blood pressure control.

Drug X is a novel angiotensin receptor-neprilysin inhibitor (ARNi) that has shown promising 
results in Phase 2 studies, demonstrating significant blood pressure reductions with a 
favorable tolerability profile.

1.2 Study Objectives

Primary Objective:
To evaluate the efficacy of Drug X 10mg once daily compared to placebo in reducing sitting 
systolic blood pressure (SBP) from baseline to Week 12 in adult patients with essential 
hypertension.

Secondary Objectives:
- To evaluate the effect of Drug X on sitting diastolic blood pressure (DBP)
- To determine the proportion of patients achieving blood pressure control (SBP < 140 mmHg 
  and DBP < 90 mmHg)
- To evaluate the safety and tolerability of Drug X

2. STUDY DESIGN

2.1 Overall Design
This is a Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study 
conducted at approximately 50 sites across the United States and Europe.

2.2 Allocation Ratio
Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either Drug X 10mg or matching placebo.

2.3 Framework
This study is designed as a superiority trial to demonstrate that Drug X is superior to 
placebo in reducing blood pressure.

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

3.1 Inclusion Criteria
- Adult patients aged 18-75 years
- Diagnosis of essential hypertension with mean sitting SBP ≥ 150 mmHg and < 180 mmHg 
  at screening and randomization
- Mean sitting DBP ≥ 90 mmHg and < 110 mmHg at screening and randomization
- Willing and able to provide written informed consent

3.2 Exclusion Criteria
- Secondary hypertension
- Severe hypertension (SBP ≥ 180 mmHg or DBP ≥ 110 mmHg)
- History of myocardial infarction, stroke, or heart failure within 6 months
- Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) < 30 mL/min/1.73m²
- Known hypersensitivity to study drug components
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women

4. INTERVENTIONS

4.1 Study Drug
Drug X 10mg oral tablet administered once daily in the morning with or without food.

4.2 Comparator
Matching placebo tablet administered once daily.

4.3 Duration of Treatment
12 weeks of double-blind treatment followed by a 2-week safety follow-up period.

5. RANDOMIZATION

5.1 Sequence Generation
Randomization will be performed using an interactive web response system (IWRS) with 
computer-generated randomization sequence prepared by the study statistician.

5.2 Stratification
Randomization will be stratified by baseline SBP category (< 160 mmHg vs ≥ 160 mmHg) 
and geographic region (North America vs Europe).

5.3 Allocation Concealment
Treatment allocation will be concealed through the IWRS system, which will assign a 
unique treatment kit number to each patient.

6. BLINDING

6.1 Blinding Procedures
This is a double-blind study. Patients, investigators, study site personnel, and sponsor 
personnel directly involved in study conduct will be blinded to treatment assignment.

6.2 Maintaining the Blind
Study drug and placebo will be identical in appearance, packaging, and labeling to 
maintain the blind.

7. OUTCOMES

7.1 Primary Outcome
Change in sitting SBP from baseline to Week 12, measured using an automated, validated 
oscillometric blood pressure monitor.

7.2 Secondary Outcomes
- Change in sitting DBP from baseline to Week 12
- Proportion of patients achieving blood pressure control at Week 12
- Time to achieve blood pressure control

8. SAFETY ASSESSMENTS

8.1 Adverse Events
Adverse events will be collected from the time of informed consent through the safety 
follow-up visit. Events will be classified using MedDRA coding dictionary.

8.2 Serious Adverse Events
Any adverse event that results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalization, 
results in persistent disability, or is a congenital anomaly will be classified as serious.

9. STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

9.1 Sample Size
A sample size of 200 patients (100 per arm) will provide 90% power to detect a difference 
of 8 mmHg in mean SBP change between groups, assuming a standard deviation of 15 mmHg 
and a two-sided alpha of 0.05. Accounting for 20% dropout, 240 patients will be enrolled.

9.2 Analysis Populations
- Intent-to-treat (ITT) population: All randomized patients
- Per-protocol (PP) population: All ITT patients without major protocol deviations
- Safety population: All patients who received at least one dose of study drug

9.3 Primary Analysis
The primary efficacy analysis will use a mixed-model repeated measures (MMRM) approach 
with treatment, visit, treatment-by-visit interaction, stratification factors, and 
baseline SBP as covariates.

9.4 Missing Data
Missing data will be handled using multiple imputation under the missing-at-random 
assumption as a sensitivity analysis.

10. ETHICS

10.1 Institutional Review Board
The protocol and all study-related materials will be reviewed and approved by the 
Institutional Review Board/Ethics Committee at each participating site.

10.2 Informed Consent
Written informed consent will be obtained from all patients prior to any study procedures.

10.3 Declaration of Helsinki
This study will be conducted in accordance with the ethical principles of the Declaration 
of Helsinki and applicable regulatory requirements.
